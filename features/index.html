Chat UX, email infrastructure

Every feature you love about Slack — channels, threads, reactions, presence — built on standard email protocols so it works with everyone.

Channels that map to email

Every channel is backed by a mailing list or shared label. Create #design, #eng, or #clients — messages route to the right people via standard email protocols.

  • Create unlimited channels per workspace
  • Each channel maps to a mailing list address
  • Invite external people by email — no sign-up needed
  • Archive channels without losing email history

Threaded conversations

Reply in-thread and it becomes an email thread with proper In-Reply-To headers. No more losing context across dozens of disconnected forwards and CCs.

  • Threads map to email threading (RFC 5322)
  • Collapse or expand threads like Slack
  • External replies land in the correct thread
  • Full thread history preserved in any email client

Works with everyone

Send a message in Woof and it arrives as a normal email for recipients not using Woof. They reply from Gmail or Outlook and it appears in your channel. No walled garden.

  • Recipients see a clean, formatted email
  • Replies automatically routed to correct channel
  • No sign-up wall for external contacts
  • Full compatibility with any email provider
